A Novel Hybrid Deep Learning Model Using U-Net and Vision Transformer for Polyp Segmentation in Medical Imaging

Principal Author: Prity Rani Das, et al.

Colorectal polyp segmentation plays a pivotal role in early endoscopic oncology screening, yet standard convolutional networks struggle with irregular mucosal margins, lighting artifacts, and scale variance. This thesis implements and validates a hybrid deep learning model unifying patch-based Vision Transformer (ViT) self-attention with the multi-scale decoding scaffold of U-Net to simultaneously preserve fine-grained edge details and global anatomical context.

Hybrid ViT + U-Net Architecture Coupled convolutional feature extraction with multi-head self-attention encoders and symmetric skip-connections.
Open Research & Reproducibility Research manuscript, trained models, and benchmark evaluation suite archived with persistent DOIs on Zenodo and GitHub.
Full Thesis Abstract: Early detection of colorectal adenomas through colonoscopy substantially reduces patient mortality. However, manual endoscopic polyp delineation remains error-prone due to mucosal variations and edge blurring. This research introduces and validates a hybrid architecture fusing Vision Transformers (ViT) with U-Net. Transformer attention mechanisms model long-range spatial context, while the U-Net convolutional decoder preserves localized edge gradients. Experimental evaluations on benchmark medical imaging datasets confirm significant reductions in false positives and heightened precision along ambiguous tissue margins.
