A Novel Hybrid Deep Learning Model Using U-Net and Vision Transformer for Polyp Segmentation in Medical Imaging

Prity Rani Das, et al.

Proposes and validates a novel hybrid deep learning architecture designed for automated, pixel-wise colorectal polyp segmentation in colonoscopy datasets. The framework unifies the high-resolution spatial feature mapping of U-Net with the global contextual representations generated by Vision Transformers (ViT), significantly reducing false positives and improving dice similarity coefficients across challenging polyp boundaries.

Full Abstract: Colorectal cancer is one of the leading causes of cancer-related mortality globally. Early detection through regular colonoscopies is crucial, yet polyp identification and boundary delineation remain susceptible to operator fatigue and complex mucosal topology. This paper introduces a novel hybrid pipeline that incorporates Vision Transformer encoders within a modified U-Net decoder scaffold. By utilizing patch-level self-attention alongside skip-connections, our model captures both micro-textures and global organ context, achieving superior performance on benchmark colonoscopy datasets compared to standard convolution baselines.

Mediscan-HybridViT: Multi-Class Medical Image Classification Using a Hybrid CNN–Transformer, DenseNet-121, and Ensemble Decision Strategies

Prity Rani Das, et al.

Developed a comprehensive multi-class medical diagnostic system integrating DenseNet-121 deep convolutional networks with Vision Transformer modules under an ensemble decision fusion strategy. The architecture systematically analyzes multi-modal medical images, mitigating gradient dispersion and achieving high diagnostic sensitivity and specificity across heterogeneous disease classes.

Full Abstract: Accurate multi-class medical diagnosis across diverse disease manifestations requires neural architectures that balance local morphological gradients with long-range topological relationships. Mediscan-HybridViT utilizes an ensemble strategy combining DenseNet-121 for continuous feature reuse with Vision Transformer blocks for multi-head global self-attention. Weighted ensemble decision logic fuses soft probabilities, demonstrating significant gains over standalone CNN baselines.

Comparative Study & Deep Learning Research Project • 2025–2026

Comparative Study of Computational Methods for Classifying 5 Types of Skin Diseases: Traditional Image Processing vs. Deep Learning

Prity Rani Das, et al.

A comprehensive benchmarking evaluation investigating the diagnostic boundary between conventional handcrafted computer vision techniques (color histograms, texture GLCM, SVM classifiers) versus modern deep convolutional neural networks for classifying dermatological lesions across 5 distinct skin disease types.

Full Abstract: Dermatological pathology requires discernment between visually indistinguishable cutaneous lesions. This study implements and compares both classical machine learning pipelines (feature extraction via GLCM, Hu moments, and SVM classification) and modern deep convolutional networks (CNNs with transfer learning). Detailed confusion matrices, ROC-AUC curves, and latency metrics demonstrate the clinical efficacy and computational tradeoffs of each methodology.

Computer Vision & Morphometrics Project Implementation • 2025–2026

Visual and Geometric Feature Extraction from Leaf Images Using Contour and Morphological Analysis

Prity Rani Das

Engineered an automated morphological feature extraction framework that performs contour segmentation, perimeter tracking, aspect ratio estimation, and structural vein analysis from biological specimen imagery, delivering high repeatability for automated botanical classification.

Full Abstract: Quantitative botanical characterization traditionally relies on labor-intensive manual geometric measurement. This project presents an algorithmic workflow leveraging digital image pre-processing, adaptive thresholding, morphological filtering, and contour tracing to compute precise geometric invariants (eccentricity, convexity, aspect ratio, solidity) from high-resolution biological leaf scans.
